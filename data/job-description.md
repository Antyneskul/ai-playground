# Senior Engineering Manager - Accounting — Shine (part of Cegid)

**Source URL:** https://careers.shine.co/jobs/7693598-senior-engineering-manager-accounting
**Retrieved:** 2026-11-04

**Department:** Product & Technology
**Locations:** Berlin, Copenhagen

## Role description

The role is about raising the team's "technical standards" and balancing ambitious technical goals with pragmatic delivery. The manager ensures reliability, security, and long-term scalability are built in from the start. The job involves joining a newly formed team, where the manager navigates shifting priorities and turns uncertainty into focused action.

## Responsibilities

- **Delivery:** "Own outcomes and help the team deliver what matters."
- **People:** "Build and grow a strong, motivated team."
- **Technical Leadership:** "you own technical outcomes, without centralising every technical decision personally."
- **Architecture & DevOps:** Bring strong judgement on testing, observability, and incident response, and take ownership of security, privacy, and data compliance. Embrace "you build it, you run it."
- **Agile:** "Help the team find the ways of working that work best for them."
- **Collaboration:** "Work closely with Product, Design, EMs and the Domain Engineer."
- **Customer Focus:** "Keep customer needs at the heart of decisions."
- **AI:** "Use AI in practical ways to improve how we build software."

## First 6–12 months

- **0–3 months:** "Form the team and build relationships." Define the mission, vision, and roadmap with the trio, set up the team for delivery, establish ways of working, and start AI adoption.
- **3–6 months:** "Deliver the first building blocks of the global accounting kernel." "Establish a sustainable delivery rhythm." Move toward AI-native engineering practices.
- **6–12 months:** "Continue delivering and evolving the accounting kernel to 9+ europeen countries." Build a high-performing team and further embed AI-native practices.

## Senior EM-specific expectations

- "Support other teams and domains."
- "Help set up or accelerate AI adoption across R&D."
- Contribute to the wider engineering organisation.

## Must-have experience

- "2+ years Engineering Management experience," ideally including a second EM role.
- "Strong technical background and ability to challenge technical decisions."
- "Full SDLC and DevOps experience: testing, observability, incident response readiness." Also security, privacy, and data compliance ownership.
- "Strong and production-proven opinions on architecture, design, testing and cloud (AWS preferred)."
- **Delivery management:** "you own the roadmap, steer timeline conversations on metrics, drive forecasting." Balance feature work with technical improvements and operations.
- **People leadership:** "run structured 1:1s, manage the career development, have direct performance conversations."
- "Comfortable with ambiguity and change."
- "Strong Agile mindset, with experience in Kanban or similar."
- "Customer-focused and able to connect technical work to customer problems."
- "Active engagement with AI beyond experimentation." Ability to critically evaluate AI's impact.
- "Discovery experience working with Product and Design."
- Specific programming languages are not mandatory.

## Nice to have

- TDD and/or BDD experience.
- Accounting or FinTech background.
